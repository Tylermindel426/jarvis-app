Jarvis

A personal assistant that posts its owner's Shorts.

Jarvis runs on its owner's own computer. With the owner's permission, it uploads and schedules short videos on the owner's own YouTube channel, Axle & Arrow, and checks back with YouTube that each one was published.

Jarvis is not a public service. Only its owner uses it, and no one else's account or data is involved.

Jarvis uses the YouTube API Services. Using it means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service.
