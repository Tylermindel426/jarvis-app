Privacy policy for Jarvis

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Jarvis is a personal assistant that only its owner uses, on the owner's own computer. This page explains what it does with data from YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

What Jarvis accesses

Jarvis gets access to an account only when its owner signs in and allows it on that platform's own sign-in screen.

  • YouTube (Google): "View your YouTube account", to read the channel's name, ID and video status, and "Manage your YouTube videos", to upload and schedule videos.
  • Instagram: basic profile information (username and account ID) and permission to publish content, used to post Reels.
  • TikTok: basic profile information (username, display name and account ID) and permission to upload videos, used to send videos to the account's drafts or post them.

Jarvis uses this access only to post the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts, and to confirm they were posted. It does not read anyone's followers, messages or other people's content. If Jarvis ever needs more, the platform's sign-in screen lists it before anything is granted.

What Jarvis stores

The permissions (access tokens) are stored only on the owner's computer, outside any shared or backed-up folder. Jarvis also keeps a local record of each post: the post ID, title and time. Jarvis has no server, so nothing is stored anywhere else.

What Jarvis shares

Nothing. Jarvis does not sell, share or transfer data to anyone. Data only moves between the owner's computer and the platform being posted to.

YouTube API Services

Jarvis uses the YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time:

  • Google/YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Instagram: Settings, then Website permissions, then Apps and websites
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy, then Security, then Manage app permissions

Deleting the token files on the owner's computer also ends Jarvis's access. Videos already posted stay up until they are removed on the platform.

Contact

The developer's contact email is shown on Google's sign-in screen for Jarvis.
